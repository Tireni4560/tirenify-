Why Tirenify exists

Cybercrime is rising across Africa faster than the tools built for it.

Online scams, phishing, SIM swaps, credential theft and business email compromise are everyday threats for African internet users. Most global security products are built for broad markets, not for the realities of the region. Tirenify exists to close that gap — first with breach awareness, then with monitoring that keeps users ahead of new threats.

30%+ of reported crime in parts of Africa is cybercrime.
