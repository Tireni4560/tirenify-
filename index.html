The product

Security hub.

Our first product is a security hub with an email breach checker. The checker, Breach Guard, shows whether your email has appeared in a known breach, which service was involved, what information may be exposed, and what to do next. Alongside it are everyday security tools that run on your device, such as a password generator, a password strength checker and QR code tools.

How alerts work

Weekly checks, only when something new appears.

Opt in on your chosen day of the week. We check your email against known breaches weekly and email you only when something new appears.

Privacy and trust

Your email is handled with care.

Emails entered for a one-off breach check are not stored — they're sent to XposedOrNot and discarded. Emails opted into alerts are stored separately, solely to run scheduled weekly checks and send results. No unsubscribe link is included in alert emails; removal is handled on request. No encryption or security measures are claimed beyond what the code supports.
