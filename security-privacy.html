Security & Privacy

How we protect you while you check.

What we collect

The email you type is used to run the check and isn’t stored. If you send us a message or use the “What we build next” form, we receive what you type (name, email, message or topic) so we can reply or contact you.

What we don’t do

We don’t ask for your password, and there are no accounts.

Encryption in transit

The site is served over HTTPS.

Third parties

This site loads its fonts from Google Fonts and its icon library (Font Awesome) from the cdnjs CDN. The homepage also uses Google Analytics to measure traffic. None of these receive the email address you check.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask us for access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold about you, ask us to restrict or stop processing it, and complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. Email support@tirenify.app to make a request.

Responsible disclosure

Found a vulnerability? Email security@tirenify.app.
